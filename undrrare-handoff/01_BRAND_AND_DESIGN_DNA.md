## 1. Identity

### ÜNDRRARE **[ESTABLISHED]**
The parent universe and the store (`undrrare.shop`). Contains both ÜNDR and
HOSHOKU. In the theme's world-token system it is treated as sharing the ÜNDR core
palette — *"per CLAUDE.md the core palette is what both identities have in
common."* **[ESTABLISHED — CODE]**

### ÜNDR — the world **[ESTABLISHED]**
> "ÜNDR sounds like something you discovered, not something that was advertised
> to you. It assumes the audience is intelligent enough to understand the feeling
> before understanding the lore."

- **Core principle:** don't sell the clothing, sell the world surrounding it.
- **If ÜNDR were a person:** *"the one standing in the corner of the party who
  somehow knows everyone, says very little, and leaves before anyone figures out
  who they were. Not trying to be mysterious — just doesn't feel obligated to
  explain themselves."*
- **Mix:** 70% underground/rebellious, 15% dark humor, 10% mysterious/cryptic,
  5% sentimental.
- **The acronym** **[OBSERVED — live Ü-000 collection copy]**:
  **Ü.NITED N.EVER D.IVIDED R.E:** — the trailing colon is as written in the
  store. The Ü-000 "BOX LOGO" product copy adds: *"That's what the four letters
  mean. The box logo is just those letters, squared off and put on a chest — no
  character, no realm, no file that needs explaining."*
- **Brand line** **[OBSERVED]**: *"If it ain't ÜNDR, I'm over it. That was a
  caption before it was a shirt."*
- **Ü is for unisex** **[OBSERVED]** — *"this is cut for everybody."*
- **Origin** **[OBSERVED]**: first batch released **September 2023**.

### HOSHOKU — the character **[ESTABLISHED]**
> "Where ÜNDR feels like a world you discover, Hoshoku feels like a character you
> meet. Hoshoku should read like a character with an actual personality — not a
> mascot being used to sell clothing."

- **Is:** cute, mischievous, expressive, warm, strange, playful, slightly
  chaotic, emotionally intelligent, curious, occasionally unsettling, never
  sterile.
- **Form** **[OBSERVED — live product copy]**: split down the middle, *not* front
  to back. Rabbit on one side (red eye, long lop ear lined in soft pink), fox on
  the other (pale blue eye, upright black-edged ear with brown inner panel),
  joined by *"a black cross-stitch seam that runs the full height of the crown —
  the same surgical seam Hoshoku wears everywhere else in the archive."*
- **Origin form** **[OBSERVED]**: chibi — *"fox half and rabbit half, drawn small
  and round before the character grew into the rest of the archive."*

#### The two personalities **[ESTABLISHED]**
Write them as **two characters interacting**, never one blended voice.

- **🐰 Bunni** — sweet, feminine, energetic, affectionate, playful, easily
  excited, slightly chaotic. May use "hehe," "omg," "wait," "look!!," ":3," "♡,"
  cute emojis, excited punctuation, playful repetition.
  **Do not make her babyish.** *"She's cute because she's expressive, not because
  she talks like a toddler."*
- **🦊 Renard** — cool, sarcastic, protective, confident, slightly mischievous,
  restrained, dryly funny. Doesn't need many words.

Canonical exchange:
> **Bunni:** I made something cute!! ♡
> **Renard:** You made a mess.
> **Bunni:** a cute mess!!
> **Renard:** ...sure.

### The relationship between ÜNDR and HOSHOKU **[ESTABLISHED]**
> **ÜNDR = the world.** Underground, detached, cryptic, confident. Sells the
> world, not the product.
> **Hoshoku = the character/soul inside the world.** Expressive, warm,
> mischievous.
> Products are physical evidence the world exists.

**The golden question, asked before writing anything:** *"Is this coming from the
world, or from the character?"* If both are present (a drop, a collab), read both
voice files and keep them **distinct within the same piece** — ÜNDR establishes
atmosphere, HOSHOKU provides personality. **Never blend them into one tone.**

Reflected structurally on the homepage as the **world-split** section
**[OBSERVED — live]**:

| | HOSHOKU side | ÜNDR side |
|---|---|---|
| Eyebrow | "Bright side" | "Dark side" |
| Body | "Playful, strange, colorful, collectible. Half rabbit, half fox." | "Underground, technical, archival. The other half of the universe." |
| CTA | "Enter HOSHOKU" | "Enter ÜNDR" |

## 2. The character / universe concept

**[OBSERVED — live collection copy]** The catalog is organized as numbered
"files" in an archive, each a chapter:

- **Ü-000 // CORE** — *"Where it started — September 2023, the first batch ÜNDR
  ever released. The box logo, the pieces built around it, and the Hoshoku prints
  that came out of the same season."*
- **Ü-001 // ENLIGHTENMENT** — *"about reaching outward — three realms, one
  origin form, and the pieces that first put Hoshoku on a garment. Hades below.
  Midgard where you stand. Nirvana where the reaching stops."*
- **Ü-002 // SEEK** — *"the awakening after the enlightenment — what comes once
  reaching for something outside yourself stops being enough."*
- **HANDMADE // 🐰/🦊** — *"Not print-on-demand. Crocheted by hand, one at a
  time, made to order."* Two files: HOSHOKU-01 (cocoa/cream, the origin) and
  HOSHOKU-02 (orange/cream).

The three realms — **Hades / Midgard / Nirvana** — are a real, live piece of
worldbuilding with products attached. The Ü-002 product copy is notably personal:
*"I made this line while I was rebuilding what I believed... You don't have to
know any of that to wear it. It's a good graphic tee either way."* That last move
— offering the lore and then releasing the reader from it — is a strong example
of the brand's actual register.

**[UNKNOWN]** The fuller mythology (what Hoshoku *is*, the origin story, how the
realms connect). The Story page says only: *"Long before there was ÜNDR, there
was Hoshoku — a creature divided between instinct and compassion. Neither hero.
Neither villain. Only balance."* — and then declares itself a placeholder.

## 3. Visual language

**[ESTABLISHED — CODE]** ÜNDRRARE is the parent universe; HOSHOKU and ÜNDR are
visual **worlds** within it, selected by a `data-world` attribute on `<html>`.
Each world defines the same set of semantic tokens.

- `[data-world="undr"]` — *"technical/editorial/archive, the current default"*
- `[data-world="hoshoku"]` — *"colorful/surreal/character-driven"*

Other live visual signatures:

- **The reticle / bullseye marker** on each world object — outer translucent
  ring, inner solid accent dot. *"echoes the reticle/bullseye visual language
  already built for the hotspot system... to signal 'there's something to
  discover here.'"*
- **The "discovered object" framing** — a soft accent glow behind an image plus a
  thin hairline frame, *"spotlit in a space, not pasted on a page."*
- **Atmosphere layering** — a heavily darkened, desaturated real product image
  behind PRESS START (`grayscale(0.65) brightness(0.32) contrast(1.05)`, opacity
  0.6) plus a faint inline-SVG noise texture at 0.05 opacity, `mix-blend-mode:
  overlay`. Type stays dominant.
- **Grain** is documented in `CLAUDE.md` as **approved but unbuilt**
  **[ESTABLISHED — CODE]**; the PRESS START noise layer is the first instance.
- **Fixed 4:5 portrait frames** for world objects, so a row of objects reads as
  comparable artifacts rather than a misaligned grid.
- **Zero border radius** on buttons and product cards; 2px on inputs.
  **[ESTABLISHED — live theme settings]**

## 6. Photography / rendering direction

**[ESTABLISHED — CODE]** Real product photography is always rendered **sharp and
in front**. Generated world renders sit **behind**, blurred and treated as
atmosphere:

> "The shared blur/gradient treatment keeps a render reading as atmosphere behind
> the live product/collection photo (still rendered sharp, unchanged, in front),
> not a second competing image."

**[ESTABLISHED — CODE]** The generation pipeline is explicitly staged:
**temporary Meshy render → later a Higgsfield render, swapped one-for-one with no
markup change.** Both slots exist today and are empty.

**[UNKNOWN]** The owner referenced known **resolution, file-size, and letterform**
problems with generated world assets. None of those assets are in the store's
file library or wired into the theme, and no notes about them survived. **Ask.**
(Letterform problems are a classic generative-image failure — text in a render
coming out as near-miss glyphs. If ÜNDR/HOSHOKU wordmarks were being generated
rather than composited, that is very likely the issue, and the fix is to
composite real type over the render rather than generate it. **[RECOMMENDATION]**)

## 7. Emotional tone

**[ESTABLISHED]**

- **ÜNDR:** cool, detached, confident, dryly funny. Short sentences and
  fragments. Lowercase where it fits. Casual profanity is allowed — *"buy some
  shit"* is a sanctioned CTA — but **never shouted**. *"The difference is
  effortlessness — ÜNDR doesn't scream."*
- **HOSHOKU:** expressive, personal, warm. Emojis yes (🐰 ♡ 🦊 :3 >:3).
  Exclamation marks yes. Profanity rare. **Not required to be happy** — Hoshoku
  can move cute → chaotic → curious → sad → mysterious → comforting →
  mischievous. A sad Hoshoku moment *"should stay sad and human — it should NOT
  suddenly pivot into generic inspirational content."*

## 8. Streetwear / underground influence **[ESTABLISHED]**

The audience overlaps: streetwear, nightlife, rave culture, anime/otaku culture,
gaming, music, art, graphic design, alternative fashion, niche internet
subcultures. **These are overlapping signals, not requirements.**

> "The defining trait isn't age, occupation, or specific subculture. It's their
> **relationship with discovery and taste.** They want to feel like they found
> something before everyone else did."

Status here is **not** luxury status: *"less 'look how expensive this is,' more
'look what I found.'"* Store tags corroborate the positioning: *atlanta
streetwear, black owned streetwear, anime streetwear, y2k streetwear, otaku
fashion, undergroundfashion, indie brand, rave wear, spiritual streetwear.*

## 10. What makes the brand feel authentic **[ESTABLISHED]**

- Restraint. Saying less than you could.
- Real facts: real materials, real production notes, real prices, real
  limitations. The beanie copy earns its mystique with *"every ear sits at a
  slightly different angle. that is the file, not a flaw."*
- Genuine rarity, stated only when true.
- Personality and human imperfection over corporate polish. *"we made another
  one." / "probably shouldn't have made this." / "we're not explaining it."*
- Letting the audience be curious without answering.
- Products that feel like things the character owns or helped make.

## 11. What makes something feel generic / corporate / AI-generated / off-brand **[ESTABLISHED]**

Named explicitly in the brand files as things to avoid:

- "Elevate your style," "premium quality," "designed for the modern individual,"
  "step into your power," "unleash your potential."
- Generic motivational language; corporate "Gen Z" language.
- Forced slang; trying too hard to sound edgy.
- **Pretending to be underground.** *"If the brand has to keep saying 'we're
  underground,' it isn't."*
- Fake exclusivity, fake urgency, countdowns, "only a few left," "LIMITED!!!"
- Over-explaining lore; explaining every piece of it.
- A paragraph underneath every image.
- Influencer-style selling; obvious sales funnels.
- **Calling everything "archive."** *"archive concepts can exist as
  worldbuilding, but shouldn't become ÜNDR's whole identity."*
- Babyish Bunni; cute language in literally every sentence; one blended
  mascot voice instead of two characters.

**The named formula failure mode:** *"If a rewrite is only reaching for the
familiar words/formatting to 'sound more on-brand,' that's the formula failure
mode — fix the attitude and reasoning behind the line instead, not just which
brand words appear in it."*

## 12. Brand voice — practical rules **[ESTABLISHED]**

### Copy formulas
- **ÜNDR:** ATTITUDE → WORLD → PRODUCT. *Never* PRODUCT → FEATURES → SALE.
- **HOSHOKU:** character reaction/interaction first, product info after.

### ÜNDR product copy structure
```
01 — HOOK      one striking sentence
02 — IDENTITY  why this piece exists in the ÜNDR world
03 — DETAILS   actual material, fit, construction
04 — CLOSING   a short line with personality
```

### CTA language
| Instead of | ÜNDR uses | HOSHOKU uses |
|---|---|---|
| Shop Now | "buy some shit" | "come see ♡" / "take one home?" |
| Discover the collection | "see what's here" | "you found us!!" |
| Explore the collection | "enter" | "come hang out." |
| Learn More | "look closer" | "which one would you choose?" |

**Important carve-out:** *"Functional ecommerce UI can stay clear and normal —
brand copy surrounding it keeps the personality."* Do not make checkout,
filters, or cart cryptic.

### Confirmed-good reference copy
- Beanie restock line: *"back for now. do what you want with that."*
- Drop email:
  > THE NEW DROP.
  >
  > you're getting this because you're already in.
  >
  > new pieces.
  > same world.
  >
  > no big speech. no countdown.
  > just some new shit to look at.
  >
  > see what's here.

Note what these do *not* do: no scarcity claim, no "archive" framing, no
persona vocabulary planted as a brand signal.

### Social voice
Shorter than expected. *"The image should do most of the explaining — the image
carries the mythology, the caption provides the attitude."*

## 13. UX philosophy

**[ESTABLISHED — CODE]** Enforced in the built code, not just aspirational:

- **Progressive enhancement, always.** Every world object is a real `<a href>`.
  JS only intercepts when the destination genuinely exists on the page;
  otherwise the real link navigates. No JS = a working, if plainer, site.
- **Never a trap.** PRESS START is skippable, session-limited, Escape-closable,
  auto-skipped under reduced-motion and in the theme editor, and never removes or
  hides the real content beneath it.
- **Never a fabricated destination.** *"No invented destinations: the object
  resolves to exactly one of a real product, a real collection, or a manually
  configured real URL... title/image/price are read live from whichever resource
  is assigned, never hardcoded."*
- **Never a placeholder graphic.** The no-image world object *"shows the object's
  real title typographically — not a fabricated or placeholder graphic."*
- **Additive-only enhancements.** New capabilities arrive as optional settings
  that render byte-identically when unset.
- **Accessibility is not deferred.** Focus is moved deliberately on boot dismiss
  and on hub scroll; a temporary `tabindex="-1"` is removed on blur; contrast was
  measured in a real browser and documented.

## 14. World-building philosophy **[ESTABLISHED]**

- **Lore is felt before it's explained.** Not *"Hoshoku is an ancient duality
  spirit originating from..."* but:
  > two sides.
  > one little creature.
  > somehow still causing problems.
- **Character first, lore second.** The audience enjoys Hoshoku first and gets
  curious about mythology second.
- Deeper mythology stays underneath the surface.
- Discovery mechanics the audience responds to: symbols, characters, visual
  clues, strange phrases, hidden details, unexplained imagery, recurring motifs,
  small pieces of lore. *"Not everything needs an explanation — 'wait… what is
  that?' is a valuable reaction."*

## 15. Identity locks / creative constraints **[ESTABLISHED]**

Non-negotiable, in the owner's own framing:

1. **ÜNDR and HOSHOKU are not the same voice.** Identify which one before
   writing. If both, keep them distinct in the same piece.
2. **VOICE ≠ VOCABULARY.** Never chase on-brand-ness by planting brand words.
3. **Scarcity claims must be confirmed facts** about the specific item. Not
   inferred, not implied, not added because it fits.
4. **Never say the brand is underground.** Let the work carry it.
5. **Don't over-explain.** Confidence is saying less.
6. **Don't force the voice onto content that can't carry it.** *"If a request
   would only make sense as generic marketing copy... say so rather than forcing
   the voice onto it."*
7. **Bunni is expressive, not babyish. Contrast is mandatory** — Bunni's energy
   against Renard's restraint.
8. **Don't pattern-match to past examples.** *"If a piece leans on the same
   handful of tricks every time, it's reading as a template, not a voice — cut
   back rather than pattern-match."*

## 16. Meaning > trend **[ESTABLISHED]**

The audience values *"authenticity over polish, taste over trend-following,
discovery over advertising, identity over status, genuine rarity over
manufactured scarcity, subculture over mainstream culture, personality over
corporate perfection, mystery over over-explanation, participation over passive
consumption."*

The motivating sequence — treat this as the brand's thesis:

> "I wasn't looking for another clothing brand."
> "I just happened to find this."
> "I don't completely understand it yet."
> "But I like it."
> "And I want to see where this goes."

> "That progression — accidental discovery → curiosity → attraction →
> not-yet-full-understanding → wanting to follow it further — is the actual
> motivator. It is NOT urgency, NOT a discount, NOT a hard sell. Copy that tries
> to close the sale by pressuring or fully explaining short-circuits this
> motivation."

And the audience principle:

> "ÜNDR never communicates like its audience is waiting to be convinced. The
> audience should feel like they discovered something already in motion. The
> brand doesn't chase the audience — it creates something interesting enough for
> the right people to notice."

## 17. Self-check before delivering any creative work **[ESTABLISHED]**

1. Could this have been written by a generic clothing brand? → rewrite if yes.
2. Does ÜNDR/HOSHOKU have an actual personality here? → rewrite if no.
3. Does it build the world rather than merely advertise the product? → rewrite if no.
4. Would the audience know who's speaking without the logo? → good if yes.
5. **ÜNDR only:** confident enough to say less? Could the sentence exist without
   mentioning the product and still feel like ÜNDR?
6. **HOSHOKU only:** is there contrast? Does it read as two characters, not one
   mascot?
7. Discovery or advertisement? → must be discovery.
8. Any unconfirmed scarcity/urgency? → remove it.
