## Motion concepts (5 s, 9:16, loopable; one action each, approved traits only)
1. **Ear lift:** Bunni's floppy ear lifts and settles.
2. **Seam glance:** eyes shift toward the centre seam, then back.
3. **Asymmetric blink:** left (red) closes, right (cyan) stays open one beat longer.
4. **Fang flash:** Renard's side smirks, small fang shows.
5. **Tear:** one tear on Bunni's side ("Sad-Bunny Tear" panel), Renard side stays dry.
6. **Deadpan stare:** both sides hold still, only the ear tips move.
