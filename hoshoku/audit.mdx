## 4. Higgsfield API status

Checked via `balance`, `list_workspaces`, `get_workflow_instructions`, `list_websites` — no generation calls were made.

- **Plan: Free. Credits: 0.** Nothing can be generated on Higgsfield right now without a purchase.
- One private workspace, no team workspaces.
- No websites created yet (`list_websites` → empty).
- Higgsfield ships a large **workflow library** (bundled SKILL.md-style playbooks), independent of credits, including: `ad-multiplier`, `brand-asset-creation`, `character-sheet`, `faceless-video`, `narrator`, `product-photoshoot`, `subtitles`, `thumbnail-generation`, five distinct `ugc-*-video` workflows, `video-editing`, and `website-builder-flow`.
- Two of these are directly relevant to the brief and worth knowing about even before any credits are spent: **`character-sheet`** (turnarounds/model sheets — exactly the "character reference consistency" tool the manga pipeline will need) and **`website-builder-flow`** (Higgsfield can build and host a standalone website or app, separate from Shopify — relevant to §11/§22).
- Higgsfield also exposes voice creation (`create_voice`, `list_voices`), TikTok publishing, and 3D scene-building tools — all dormant (no credits, nothing built) but worth knowing they exist for the future VTuber/music path.
- **Overlap with OpenArt:** both do text/image-to-image and image/video generation. Higgsfield's edge is *workflows* (character sheets, website builder, ad/UGC pipelines, TikTok publishing) and *batch tools*; OpenArt's edge is breadth of individually selectable frontier models (Nano Banana, GPT Image, Seedream, etc.) at finer per-model control.
- **Recommendation (unchanged from the brief's own framing):** OpenArt Plus = default hands-on lab; Higgsfield = selective specialist for character sheets, website building, and batch/automation — funded with a small top-up only once a concrete task justifies it.
