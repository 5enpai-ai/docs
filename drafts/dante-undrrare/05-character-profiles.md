# 5. Character-format profiles

These profiles come from each character's published canon: the *Hunter × Hunter* manga and 2011 anime, and the *Darling in the Franxx* 2018 anime. They're drawn from my knowledge of those works, not from a fetched source (the wikis are blocked here), so please check any line you plan to rely on. **Nothing here comes from the Dante videos or from HOSHOKU canon.**

The "Where the product can enter" rows pair a canon trait with a verified garment property from [`04-product-identity-map.md`](04-product-identity-map.md) §4.3. They're possibilities, not jokes. Which one becomes the callback waits on the Dante analysis.

## Guardrails that apply to both characters

- **No sexualized framing of either character.** Killua is a child (12 at the start of the series). Zero Two's age is canonically ambiguous and she looks teenaged. Don't pair Killua with swimwear. Pairing Zero Two with the `"Seek" Bikini` is not recommended (→ open questions).
- **Brand language rule:** "No predator/prey or hunting language". That collides with *Hunter* × Hunter, Killua's assassin background and Zero Two's "partner killer" reputation. Scripts must lean on other traits (→ open questions).
- **Keep the character recognizable:** hair, eyes, horns and silhouette should match canon. The garment is reproduced exactly from the reference art and never redesigned (Brand Rules › Image generation 1–2).

## 5.1 Killua Zoldyck (*Hunter × Hunter*)

**Gender correspondence:** male.

| Area | Canon basis | Format use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core identity | Third son of the Zoldyck family of professional assassins. Trained since infancy, and walked away from the family business. | He treats extreme situations as mundane. That's his deadpan register. |
| Personality | Cool, cocky and teasing on the surface; loyal and easily embarrassed when sincerity is required. He calls Gon an idiot and still follows him anywhere. | A split between cool front and flustered reaction is his reliable beat. |
| Signature habits | Big sweet tooth, especially Chocorobo-kun chocolate. Rides a skateboard. Carries heavy steel yo-yos. | Props and wants that can drive a premise |
| Signature physical comedy | The anime's "cat face" (":3" mouth, half-lidded eyes) when he's scheming or smug. Hands turn into claws. | A recurring visual reaction slot |
| Abilities | Resistant to poison and electricity (torture training). His Transmutation Nen turns aura into electricity: *Godspeed* ("Whirlwind", "Speed of Lightning"). *Rhythm Echo* makes afterimages. | Being too fast or too many at once. Casual overkill. |
| Typical reactions | Bored by tests he finds easy. Blunt corrections. Walks away mid-conversation. | Short, flat lines. Few words. |
| Deadpan opportunities | Answers ordinary questions with assassin-casual facts. Is unimpressed by danger and very impressed by candy. | Contrast of scale |
| Situation types that fit the character | Anything with a trivial test, a queue, a snack, or a friend being dramatic | For premise selection |

## 5.2 Zero Two / 02 (*Darling in the Franxx*)

**Gender correspondence:** female.

| Area | Canon basis | Format use |

| Personality | Playful, impulsive, teasing, openly defiant of rules. Underneath she's lonely and wants to be seen as human rather than a "monster". | A tease-then-sincere shift is her reliable beat |

| Comedic contrast | Menacing reputation versus childlike glee over sweets. Horns and a fearsome image in ordinary settings. | Contrast of image and behavior |
