- Brand rules that apply to video work:
  - Reproduce garment graphics exactly. Don't let AI regeneration redesign a print.

## 4.3 Properties of the product that a script can build on

These are facts about the garments, not jokes. They're the raw material the ÜNDRRARE format can point the joke back toward.

1. **The two shirts are a designed pair.** The store copy says so directly ("One half of a pair" / "The other half"). The Truth front carries a canine; the Reality front carries a rabbit.
2. **The Seek Truth shirt says `SEEK TRUTH` on the front and `SEEK` / `REALITY` on the back.** So the wearer shows one word from the front and the other from behind.
3. **The back text is set so that it changes with orientation.** Right side up, the top arc reads `SEEK` and the bottom arc is upside down. Rotated 180°, `REALITY` reads right side up and `SEEK` flips. What the viewer can read depends on how they look at it.
4. **Casing differs:** `TRUTH` is set in capitals and `Reality` in title case on the fronts.
5. **Every front carries the word `SEEK`.** It's the constant across the pair. Only the second word changes.

Whether the recurring callback should use one of these properties is decided **after** the Dante brand-recall analysis (§3.2). For now they're listed as options only.
