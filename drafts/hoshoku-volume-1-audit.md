## B. The existing blueprint (as recorded, not rewritten)

The only written blueprint is the Notion Brand Bible plus the PROJECTS entry. No chapter plan, page script, or panel list for Volume 1 was found anywhere. If a fuller blueprint was set out in an earlier chat, it was never saved to a connected tool.

### Already decided or approved (CANON)

1. Series **HOSHOKU**, Volume 1 **"Flashback"**. Chapter 1 released on Webtoon in June 2026.
2. **Vertical-scroll webtoon format.** All art is recomposed vertically and never inherits the source aspect ratio.
3. Origin: a boot-camp dream from around 2024.
4. **Broly**: protagonist, time-rewind "**bookmark**", with a mental-strain cost tracked by the **ODDITY meter**.
5. **"The 3"**: Broly, **Nason** (elemental skin/material manipulation), **Juniko / Niko** (random power manifestation).
6. Side characters **Gatkey** (gang leader) and **McNeal**. Neither is a main antagonist.
7. Arc 1 antagonist: **the storekeeper** (porcupine powers).
8. HOSHOKU is a **figment of Broly's imagination**, imperceptible to others. The "others can perceive him" reveal is **several volumes ahead** and out of Volume 1 scope.
9. **HOSHOKU design lock:** anthro rabbit/fox fusion, age 18, 175 cm, exact 50/50 vertical split, surgical-stitch seam (plus shoulders, wrists, knees), athletic build, clawed paws, dark grey shorts baseline, true animal muzzle (not a human face with accessories).
10. Personality: Bunni side sad/vulnerable, Renard side cocky/playful. Overall playful yet melancholic.
11. Art style: rough dry-brush ink, cel-shaded, high detail.
12. **Lettering system:** Bunni red, Renard blue, split bubble when fused, distinct internal-thought and per-character bubbles.
13. Typography direction: bold condensed display for SFX, clean rounded sans for dialogue. **No family has been chosen.**
14. Process rules: **Canva and existing assets before credits**. Cite the reference used. Say so when no reference exists. **Never invent lore.** Flag conflicts rather than resolve them.

### Continuity flags (observed, not resolved)

1. **Broly's hair.** The reference sheet says pink-and-blonde ombré dreads. Pages 1–4 and 16/17/19 show short blonde twists. Pages 5–14 and 18 show pink/red or ombré dreads. This might be a deliberate pre-lab/post-lab change, or it might be drift. You need to decide which.
2. **Claymation origin.** In the claymation, Pai stitches separate rabbit and fox puppets into HOSHOKU. Manga canon only says HOSHOKU exists in Broly's imagination. The claymation origin stays **ADAPTATION** until you confirm it for the manga.
3. **Lettering-guide sample lines** ("Something's not right here…", "I've seen it before.") are **style examples**. They are not scripted dialogue.
4. **Page 14's numbered reflections (`#007`, `#614`, `#389`)** may connect to the multiverse identity, but no source says so. They are left uninterpreted.
