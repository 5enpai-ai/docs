## B. Existing manga story (as built)

**Chapter 1: "FLASHBACK — RECRUITMENT DAY"** (12 spreads, ~50 panels, lettered, published)

1. Recruitment day at sunset, recruiting center. *"No turning back now."*
2. Bootcamp montage, Day 8 → Day 34: pool, rope wall, crawl, boxing gym, rifle range. *"Breathe. Don't panic." "No excuses." "Just… one more round." "Two forward squads—"*
3. Day 8 barracks, 02:13 AM courtyard, breakdown in the rain. *"Can't… slow down…" "Just… breathe." "I'm gonna—"* An unidentified person grabs his arm: *"Whoa! Easy—"*
4. PRE-BLACKOUT: injection. *"Hold still."* beep… drip… fade.
5. Black. *"Day unknown. Location unknown." "ngh…" "Where… am I…?"* He wakes bandaged.
6–7. Military Medical Wing: masked figure, syringe, blacking in and out, staff looming.
8–9. Corridor, gurney, IV. A lab of tanks: Broly suspended in a tank with a breathing mask. Vials, monitors.
10. Alarm, sparks, armed personnel running.
11–12. Hand on the glass, the tank shatters, white-out silhouette (breakout).

**Chapter 2** (opening only, ~9 panels, no dialogue)

13. Broly wakes at home in a sweat, walks the hallway, washes his hands.
14. Mirror. A butterfly-clock motif. Numbered reflections of Broly: `#007`, `#614`, `#389`.

What the art does **not** yet show:

- HOSHOKU / Bunni / Renard (the emblem strip only)
- The bookmark ability
- The ODDITY meter
- Nason, Niko, Gatkey, McNeal, or the storekeeper
