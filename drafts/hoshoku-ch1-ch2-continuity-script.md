# HOSHOKU Vol 1 "Flashback": Chapter 1 + Chapter 2 opening, as-built continuity reference

Compiled 2026-09-26. Zero credits. Manga material only; no claymation material or evidence is used anywhere.

This document **transcribes and describes what exists**. It adds no dialogue, beats, or explanations.

## Method and limits

**Primary source.** Canva design `FLASHBACK – RECRUITMENT DAY` (`DAG9IhSY4Xo`), 19 pages. Comparison source: `HOSHOKU — Chapter 1 Webtoon Panels` (`DAHR7QwZmf8`).

**Panel-to-asset mapping.** Read from the design's element data. An editing session was opened read-only and **cancelled without saving**, so the design is unchanged. Asset names come from the Canva `Hoshoku` folder.

**Visual reads.** Made from Canva page thumbnails (500×400 per spread, 334×598 per tall page), enlarged locally. PNG export of this design is refused for this account, so no higher-resolution source was available. Colour and small-text reads are marked **UNVERIFIED** where resolution limits certainty.

**Where the text lives.** Most Chapter 1 dialogue and SFX is **baked into PNG overlays**, for example `ch01_bubbles_text_1-15_transparent`, `page2_bubbles_text_transparent`, and `sfx_*`. It is not live text. Transcriptions of baked text are visual reads. Live Canva text elements are marked `[live text]` and are exact.

**Speaker attribution.** No bubble names its speaker. Attribution is given only from tail direction or who is in frame, marked **(by tail)** or **UNVERIFIED**.

### Lettering tags

The comparison standard is the Canva *Lettering & SFX Style Guide* (`DAHR7ZC2IKs`, Aug 2026). Chapter 1 was lettered in Jan 2026, **before** that guide existed, so most Chapter 1 lettering predates it.

| Tag | Appearance in the artwork |
|---|---|
| `SPEECH-ROUND` | White oval/round bubble, thin black border, pointed tail. Not the guide's Broly bubble (thick border, one clipped corner). |
| `THOUGHT-CLOUD` | White cloud with a trailing dot chain, black upright text, no parentheses. Shape matches the guide's internal-thought bubble; colour, italic, and parentheses do not. |
| `SHOUT-BURST` | White spiky burst, black bold text |
| `SFX-WHITE` | Bold white caps with a dark outline/shadow, no container. Matches the guide's "plain bold white caps" variant. |
| `SFX-AMBIENT` | Translucent grey lowercase text with trailing dots, no container. **Not a style-guide category.** |
| `CAPTION-BOX` | White rectangle, black border, black text |
| `CAPTION-TEAL` | Dark bar with a cyan outline and cyan monospace text (page 3 only) |
| `CAPTION-TALL` | Plain box with plain sans text, used on tall pages 16–19 |
| `VOICE-LOOSE` | Text with no container |
| **Not used anywhere** | Bunni red, Renard blue, the fused split bubble, the guide's Broly bubble, crimson SFX bursts |

**Font references** (Canva internal IDs; family names are not exposed):

- `YAD_Q_TxnIk,0`: hand-lettered comic face (title caption, "ngh…", "Where… am I…?")
- `YAD_Q-gmDL8,0`: small-caps caption face ("no turning back now", "Day unknown / Location unknown")
- `YAFdJt8dAY0,1`: bold ("CH. 2")
- `YACgEZ1cb1Q,0`: plain sans (every text element on tall pages 16–19)

### Evidence status

- **ART**: visibly established by the artwork
- **CANON-NOTE**: claimed in the Notion canon, not shown in the art
- **OBSERVED**: visible, with no written explanation
- **UNVERIFIED**: cannot be confirmed at the available resolution
- **ILLEGIBLE**: cannot be read
- **CONTRADICTORY**: two sources in the material disagree
